# IMD Maximum Temperature Dataset

Dataset Name:
IMD Gridded Maximum Temperature

Source:
India Meteorological Department (IMD)

Years:
1980 - 2025

Resolution:
1.0° × 1.0°

Format:
GRD

Files:
46 (1980–2025)



Parameter:
Daily Maximum Surface Temperature (°C)

Purpose:
Used for maximum temperature prediction in AI-Powered Digital Twin of India's Climate.
